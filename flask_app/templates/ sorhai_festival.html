🌾 Sohrai Festival of Jharkhand 🎨

A harvest and cattle festival that blends gratitude, tribal traditions, and stunning wall paintings.

What is Sohrai?

Sohrai is a post-harvest and cattle festival celebrated by tribal communities in Jharkhand. It coincides with the new moon after Diwali, marking the end of harvest and honoring cattle as sacred companions of farming life.

📅 When

Right after Diwali (Oct–Nov), lasting 3–5 days.

🌾 Why

To celebrate the rice harvest, honor cattle, and express gratitude towards nature.

👥 Who

Celebrated by Santhal, Munda, Oraon, Kurmi, Agaria, and many other tribal groups.

Festival Rituals

Day 1: Preparation

Homes and cattle sheds are cleaned, walls whitewashed, and natural pigments prepared.

Day 2: Cattle Worship

Cattle are bathed, decorated with vermillion and flowers, and revered as divine beings.

Evening Rituals

Diyas are lit, offerings made to Gaurea (animal deity), and villages celebrate with songs, dances, and feasts.

Sohrai Glimpses

The Sohrai Art Tradition

Sohrai is world-renowned for its wall paintings, especially in Hazaribagh. Women use natural pigments from earth, clay, and plants to create motifs of animals, plants, and ancestral symbols on mud walls. Each color holds meaning — red for fertility, black for divinity, and white for prosperity.

Cultural & Economic Impact

  • 🎭 Preserves tribal identity and traditions
  • 🎨 Promotes indigenous Sohrai art globally
  • 🌍 Boosts eco-cultural tourism in Jharkhand
  • 📚 Transfers ancient wisdom to younger generations
Sohrai teaches us gratitude, sustainability, and harmony with nature — values deeply relevant even today.

Quick intro

Gumla is a quiet, hilly district rich in waterfalls, tribal culture and rocky outcrops. It's an offbeat destination for hikers, photographers and those seeking cultural immersion.

Practical Information

Transport

Nearest rail: Gumla station. Road connections to Ranchi and Simdega; local buses and taxis for nearby sites.

Where to stay

Basic hotels in Gumla town; homestays in villages and small guesthouses near scenic spots.

Safety & tips

Carry water and sturdy shoes for trails, avoid slippery rocks near falls, hire local guides for remote areas and respect tribal customs.

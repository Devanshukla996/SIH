Quick intro

Ranchi blends natural escapes with city buzz — dramatic waterfalls, breezy dams, leafy campuses and a lively food scene. Perfect for quick weekend getaways and family trips.

Practical Information

Transport

Airport: Birsa Munda (IXR). Rail: Ranchi Jn., Hatia. Roads: NH-43/NH-20. Local taxis, autos and app cabs available.

Where to stay

Hotels around Main Road, Hinoo & Kanke; lakeside stays near Kanke/Patratu valley (day trip) for nature views.

Safety & tips

Waterfalls have strong undercurrents in monsoon — heed safety boards; carry cash for rural spots; start early for Tagore Hill sunrises.

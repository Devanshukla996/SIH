Quick intro

Known as the cultural and spiritual heart of Jharkhand, Deoghar is home to the famous Baidyanath Jyotirlinga — one of the 12 holy Jyotirlingas. The town blends devotion, tradition and natural charm, with hills and caves nearby.

Practical Information

Transport

Rail: Baidyanath Dham station. Road: Connected by NH and buses. Air: Deoghar Airport (direct flights from Kolkata, Delhi). Local autos and taxis available.

Where to stay

Budget hotels, dharamshalas and lodges near temple area; modern hotels available in town and near airport.

Safety & tips

Best visited in winter and Shravan months. Expect large crowds during Shravan Mela. Keep belongings safe and respect temple guidelines.

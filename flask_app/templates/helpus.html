FAQs

How are my tips verified and used?
Submitted tips are reviewed for accuracy and safety. Once verified, we add them to visitor guidance, maps, or site notices with periodic rechecks.
Can I volunteer part-time or on weekends?
Yes. Volunteer schedules are flexible—weekends and seasonal programs are available. Induction and safety briefings are provided.
What issues should be reported immediately?
Safety hazards, wildlife harassment, damaged signage on busy trails, aggressive littering, and price fraud should be reported as soon as possible.

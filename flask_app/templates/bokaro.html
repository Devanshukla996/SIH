Quick intro

Bokaro is a planned industrial township with green parks, reservoir escapes and rich tribal culture. Ideal for short nature escapes and cultural exploration.

Practical Information

Transport

Nearest airport: Ranchi (~120 km). Rail: Bokaro Steel City Station. Road: NH-18 & NH-320; buses and taxis available.

Where to stay

Hotels and guesthouses in Bokaro Steel City; homestays near dam/hills for eco experiences.

Safety & tips

Carry water in summers, wear trekking shoes for hills, respect tribal customs, book during festivals early.

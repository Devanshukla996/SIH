Quick intro

Known as the “Mica capital of India,” Koderma is blessed with hills, forests, dams and ancient temples. A quiet district ideal for nature, culture and pilgrim tourism.

Practical Information

Transport

Rail: Koderma Junction (on Howrah–Delhi line). Air: Ranchi (160 km), Gaya (130 km). Road: NH-31 passes through Koderma.

Where to stay

Budget hotels in Koderma town; eco-lodges and guest houses near Tilaiya Dam & wildlife sanctuary.

Safety & tips

Carry essentials for treks, respect local customs, and avoid deep forest zones without guides.

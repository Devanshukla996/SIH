About this item

The Bamboo Tokri is a handwoven traditional basket crafted by tribal artisans using eco-friendly and sustainable bamboo. Known for its durability and natural beauty, it is ideal for storage, home organization, or decorative purposes.

Each basket is created using age-old weaving techniques passed down through generations, making it not just a functional piece but also a cultural artifact that connects you with the heritage of Jharkhand’s tribal communities.

Quick intro

Latehar is a lush, hilly district home to Betla National Park (Palamu Tiger Reserve), dramatic waterfalls and the famous Netarhat plateau. Perfect for wildlife safaris, treks and misty sunrises.

Practical Information

Transport

Nearest airport: Ranchi (~150 km). Rail: Latehar, Barwadih & Daltonganj (for Betla/Netarhat). Road: NH-39; taxis and buses available.

Where to stay

Forest bungalows and lodges near Betla; tourist rest houses at Netarhat; basic hotels in Latehar/Daltonganj.

Safety & tips

Book safaris in advance, carry warm layers at Netarhat winters, follow forest rules, and hire local guides for remote areas.

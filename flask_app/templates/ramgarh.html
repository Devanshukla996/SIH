Quick intro

Ramgarh district is known for lush green hills, waterfalls, and historical temples. A calm retreat for nature lovers and weekend travellers from nearby cities like Ranchi and Bokaro.

Practical Information

Transport

Nearest rail: Ramgarh (local), Ranchi (~50 km). Road: NH-33 and local buses. Taxis and autorickshaws available.

Where to stay

Guesthouses and small hotels in Ramgarh town; eco-lodges near Patratu Valley and hills for nature lovers.

Safety & tips

Best visited in winter; trek with a local guide in hills; avoid monsoon treks; respect local customs at temples.

About this item

Khovar art is a sacred mural painting tradition from Jharkhand, usually created during weddings. It symbolizes fertility, marital bliss, and new beginnings. The art is characterized by a monochrome style painted on walls using a comb technique, with white clay patterns drawn over a black earth background.

The motifs represent cosmic balance, fertility, and the union of life forces. Traditionally painted in wedding chambers, Khovar art is both decorative and deeply symbolic, carrying forward cultural heritage.

By purchasing this artwork, you are supporting local artisans and helping preserve an ancient cultural tradition.

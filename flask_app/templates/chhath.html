🌞 Chhath Puja: The Sacred Festival of Jharkhand 🌿

A spiritual journey of purity, discipline, and devotion to the Sun God, celebrated with grandeur in Jharkhand along its holy rivers.

What is Chhath Puja?

Chhath Puja is a four-day Hindu festival dedicated to the Sun God (Surya) and his consort, Chhathi Maiya. Falling six days after Diwali, it is one of the most rigorous and spiritually uplifting festivals, performed at rivers, ponds, and water bodies.

📅 When

October–November (Kartik Shukla Shashthi), 6 days after Diwali.

🌞 Why

To thank the Sun God for sustaining life and to seek blessings for family welfare.

👥 Who

Observed by devotees across Jharkhand, Bihar, and Uttar Pradesh with deep community participation.

The Four-Day Ritual Journey

Day 1: Nahay Khay

Holy bath, home purification, and a single simple meal mark the beginning of the festival.

Day 2: Lohanda/Kharna

A day-long waterless fast, broken in the evening with prasad of kheer, chapati, fruits, and jaggery.

Day 3: Sandhya Arghya

Main ritual offering to the setting sun, performed at riverbanks with baskets of fruits, Thekua, and diyas.

Day 4: Usha Arghya

The grand finale at dawn – devotees offer prayers to the rising sun, completing the 36-hour fast.

Festival Glimpses

Traditional Foods and Offerings

  • 🍪 Thekua – signature sweet made of wheat flour, jaggery, and ghee
  • 🥛 Kheer – rice pudding with jaggery and milk
  • 🥥 Coconut and jaggery laddus
  • 🍌 Seasonal fruits like bananas, sugarcane, and water chestnuts

Unique Aspects in Jharkhand

Chhath Puja in Jharkhand blends Hindu traditions with tribal influences. Major celebrations happen along the Subarnarekha, Damodar, and Kharkai rivers. Folk songs, traditional instruments, and community unity make it a vibrant spiritual experience.

Modern Relevance

Despite urbanization and climate change challenges, Chhath Puja continues to thrive with eco-friendly practices, community participation, and diaspora celebrations worldwide.

🌍 Chhath Puja reminds us of discipline, gratitude, and living in harmony with nature and community.
